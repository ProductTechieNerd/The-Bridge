# Business Brief: The Bridge

## The business
The Bridge is the young adults' arm of RCCG Victory Centre, Swindon. It has about 29 members. Each member pays £5 a month into an agreed collective account, which funds meetings and outings that happen twice a month.

## The person who uses it
Members are aged 18 to 35. Some are students and some are working. They use phones mostly, but also laptops and iPads, at home, on the go and on campus. Their connection is a mix of mobile data, home wifi and campus wifi, so pages must load fast on a weak signal. Most are comfortable with everyday apps like social media and shopping, but I will check this with the group's leaders.

## The one thing that must work
A member opens The Bridge on their phone, logs in, and within one minute knows what is happening next, where and when it is, what they need to do, and what they owe.

## Three questions the group gets asked repeatedly
1. Where are we meeting?
2. How much is it?
3. Who is coming?

Today these answers are spread across WhatsApp polls and an Excel sheet that one person holds.

## How it should feel, and why
People are in many WhatsApp groups. The volume of messages causes mental fatigue, so many members stop checking The Bridge group at all. The app should shut out that noise. It should feel calm, warm and personal, with friendly fonts and large, easy to read text on a phone. The aim is that members check it about twice a week as part of their routine.
